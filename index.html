NL Fishing Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026
Publisher: delastandevelopment
Contact: delastandevelopment@gmail.com

This policy explains what information NL Fishing ("the app") handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. It applies to the NL Fishing app for iPhone and Android.

1. Summary

  • Your spots, catches, and photos are stored on your device.
  • We do not run advertising, analytics, or tracking in the app.
  • Your location is used only when you allow it, to centre the map, tag a catch or spot, or show weather for where you are.
  • The app asks for a town's weather by sending its coordinates to our weather service. We do not link that request to your name.

2. Information the app handles

Stored on your device only

  • Fishing spots you add: name, region, water type, notes, species, optional map position, and an optional photo.
  • Catches you log: date, species, spot, length, weight, outcome, notes, an optional photo, and an optional GPS position.
  • Your choice of weather town and your "show sample data" setting.

This information is saved in the app's storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Location

The app asks for location permission to centre the map on you, to tag a catch or spot with where it was, and to show weather for your position. You can refuse permission. The app still works, and you can choose a town instead.

Photos and camera

The app asks for access to your photo library and camera so you can attach photos to catches and spots. Photos are copied into the app's own storage on your phone. The app does not upload them.

Weather requests

When you view weather, the app sends the coordinates of the chosen town or your position to our weather service, which fetches public forecasts from Environment and Climate Change Canada. These requests are not linked to your name, an account, or your device identifier. We do not keep a record of who requested which location.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not require an account to use the app's main features.

4. Future features

Some features may be added later, such as account sign-in, backup and sync of your catches, community reports, or paid features. If any of these collect or send personal information, we will update this policy before they are released, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

5. Regulations and weather information

NL Fishing shows regulations, seasons, and bag limits for planning and record-keeping only. It is not the official source. Check the Department of Fisheries and Oceans (DFO) Anglers' Guide and your licence conditions before fishing. Weather information comes from Environment and Climate Change Canada and is shown for reference. Do not rely on it alone for safety at sea.

6. Children

NL Fishing is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your choices

  • Delete a spot or catch inside the app at any time.
  • Remove the app to delete all data stored on your phone.
  • Change location and photo permissions in your phone's settings.

8. Security

Information stays on your device, protected by your phone's own security. We cannot see it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do, and we will tell you in the app for significant changes.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: delastandevelopment@gmail.com
